Wiregrass Rehabilitation Center Web System

https://github.com/ryanbrett/wrc-accessible-web

Custom accessible website architecture engineered for Wiregrass Rehabilitation Center (WRC). Focused on WCAG 2.1 AA screen reader contrast compliance, simplified navigation hierarchies for vocational training programs, and fast mobile loading times.

Source code and design specifications archived on GitHub.

Early Portfolio & Technical Sandbox

https://github.com/ryanbrett

The original sandbox repository housing experimental CSS grid architectures, Swiss modernist poster layouts translated into HTML5, and early Node.js utility scripts.

Archived repositories remain accessible under the @ryanbrett GitHub profile.

Independent Label Packaging & Vector Systems

High-resolution vector artwork, physical merchandise apparel separations, and CD/vinyl pre-press jacket layout templates for regional independent musicians and recording artists.

Covered screen-print spot color channel separations, die-line templates, and digital album release assets.

Structural Sign Mounting & CAD Library

A standardized library of 2D/3D fabrication templates for architectural monument signs, dimensional letters, and welded aluminum framing. Designed to accelerate drafting and permit documentation for commercial sign installations.

Integrated directly with Kongsberg cutting files and shop-floor welding specifications.
