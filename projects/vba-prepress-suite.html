Case Study // Industrial Automation & Pre-Press Engineering

Departmental Pre-Press Automation Suite

A comprehensive VBA software engine and custom UserForm ecosystem built to automate repetitive pre-press tasks, eliminate operator setup mistakes, and execute deterministic vector imposition across high-volume production manufacturing.

01 // Architectural Scope

Beyond Simple Variable Data Merging

While standard variable data merge utilities exist out of the box in graphic software, they are fragile and limited: they fail on complex multi-line overflows, require tedious manual bounding-box setup, cannot sanitize incoming customer data, and do not handle pre-press validation or downstream CNC toolpathing.

To solve this for an entire production department, I developed an extensive, modular VBA automation library centered around a comprehensive UserForm pre-press cockpit. The suite intercepts raw customer spreadsheets and automates every repetitive manual step between job intake, vector rendering, color governance, and digital finishing.

02 // Core Capabilities & Modules

Custom UserForm Pre-Press Hub

The custom UserForm interface acts as a single centralized control panel for all department operators:

  • Deterministic Pre-Flight Diagnostics: Scans complex vector documents in milliseconds for unclosed sub-paths, duplicate overlapping nodes, out-of-gamut RGB elements, missing spot ink definitions, and missing registration marks.
  • Automated Pre-Press Error Correction: Automatically converts unapproved spot inks to calibrated CMYK/Pantone tolerances, joins open curve segments, strips redundant clip masks, and generates uniform 0.125" production bleeds.
  • Intelligent Dynamic Imposition: Programmatically calculates optimal sheet nesting densities, gutter spacing, cut-mark registration lines, and Kongsberg CNC camera fiducials based on sheet stock dimensions.
  • RegEx Data Cleansing: Parses unstructured customer Excel manifests, automatically fixing telephone casing, address abbreviating, SKU formatting, and barcode checksums before generation.
  • Automated Proof & Vector Exporter: Compiles low-resolution customer PDF proofs and high-resolution RIP production files in a single pass with standardized naming conventions.
