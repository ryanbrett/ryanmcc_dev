Technical Background & Focus

https://github.com/ryanbrett

My background centers on technical design for large-format manufacturing, precision CNC routing (Kongsberg tables), welding/mounting schematics, and fortifying large-scale corporate print assets under rigorous brand governance.

I bridge the physical and software worlds by writing custom VBA and Python automation macros, building deterministic web tools for regulatory compliance (such as OSHA 1910.145, ANSI Z535, and Metrc RFID workflows), and engineering programmable document layouts with Typst.

Explore active projects (including web applications like signai.ryanmcc.dev, ag.ryanmcc.dev, and flora.ryanmcc.dev), inspect past builds in the archive, or check out the open-source Typst variable resume repository.

Manufacturing & Software Disciplines

Core technical competencies across physical industrial production and software development:

Technical Design & Industrial Fabrication

  • Large Format Production: Industrial RIP rasterization, spot color separations, substrate compatibility, and high-tolerance physical finishing.
  • Digital Finishing & Routing: Toolpathing and CAD/CAM setup for Kongsberg precision knife and CNC router tables.
  • Sign Mounting & Structural Schematics: Production drawings for precise welding, structural brackets, architectural sign mounting, and UL compliance.
  • Corporate Brand Governance: Enforcing strict color fidelity (Pantone/CMYK tolerances) and visual standards for multi-facility enterprise deployments.

Software & Automation Engineering

  • Front-End Architecture: React, TypeScript, Semantic HTML5, and native CSS design systems.
  • Telemetry & Real-Time APIs: Node.js, Express, environmental sensor APIs, and lightweight data streaming.
  • Departmental Pre-Press Automation: CorelDraw VBA UserForm applications, batch error correction, automated bleed/crease imposition, and variable data pipelines.
  • Document Systems: Programmable Typst document engines and algorithmic vector layout generation.

Regulatory & Safety Standards

  • Industrial Safety Panels: OSHA 1910.145 warning standards and ANSI Z535 compliant visual communication systems.
  • State Agricultural Tracking: Metrc RFID batch tracking, transfer manifests, and environmental monitoring workflows.
  • Pharmaceutical & Medical Labeling: Strict AMCC guidelines, barcode validation, and automated compliance auditing.
